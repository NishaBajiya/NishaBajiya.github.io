Research experience

Doctoral Researcher · January 2022 – January 2027

IIIT-Delhi, New Delhi · Supervisor: Prof. Gajendra P. S. Raghava

  • Built machine-learning and deep-learning predictors for peptide function and activity (antimicrobial and cell-penetrating peptides) and for therapeutic aptamers
  • Compiled and cleaned large sequence datasets from the literature and public databases for model training and benchmarking
  • Set up Python-based analysis pipelines on HPC systems to handle large-scale data processing and model training
  • Generated sequence embeddings with peptide (PepBERT), protein (ProtBERT, BioBERT, ESM-2), and nucleotide (DNABERT, Nucleotide Transformer) language models
  • Released the resulting tools as web servers, command-line software, GitHub repositories, and PyPI packages for use by bench scientists

Dissertation Research (M.Sc.) · January – June 2019

Jaipur National University, Jaipur (Rajasthan) · Supervisors: Dr. Divya Shrivastava, Dr. Smita Pareek

  • Dissertation: “Isolation, Identification and Antifungal Susceptibility Testing of Dermatophytes from Clinical Samples Obtained from a Tertiary Care Hospital in Jaipur, Rajasthan”
  • Processed clinical samples (skin scrapings, hair, nail clippings) from 60 patients; screened by KOH microscopy
  • Isolated dermatophytes on SDA and DTM; identified species by colony morphology, LPCB staining, and slide culture
  • Performed antifungal susceptibility testing by agar disk diffusion (six antifungal agents)

Dissertation Trainee (B.Sc.) · June – August 2016

Dr. B. Lal Institute of Biotechnology, Jaipur (Rajasthan) · Supervisor: Dr. Parul Chowdhury

  • Dissertation: “Tissue-specific effect of drought stress on antioxidant enzymes of two varieties of Vigna radiata”
  • Conducted pot experiment on two mung bean varieties (RMG-975, RMG-492) under mild, moderate, and severe drought stress
  • Measured root and shoot length, fresh and dry weight, and protein content (Bradford assay)
  • Assayed catalase, SOD, ascorbate peroxidase, and guaiacol peroxidase in leaf and root tissue by UV-Vis spectrophotometry
  • Completed training in plant biotechnology (1–15 June 2016)

Research Trainee · June – July 2015

Biyani Girls College, Jaipur (Rajasthan) · Supervisor: Ms. Heena Kewalramani

  • Completed training and report on electrophoretic techniques (15 days)
