Welcome

Hi, I'm Nisha.

I am currently pursuing my PhD in Computational Biology at IIIT-Delhi under the supervision of Prof. Gajendra P. S. Raghava. My research brings together artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and biological data analysis to develop computational tools and curated databases for therapeutic discovery.

My journey into computational biology began with a strong foundation in experimental biotechnology. My Master's (M.Sc. Biotechnology) research focused on the isolation, identification, and antifungal susceptibility testing of dermatophytes from clinical samples, where I gained practical experience in microbiology, molecular biology, and laboratory diagnostics. Working across both experimental and computational biology has shaped the way I approach research — using computational methods to address biological questions while staying connected to their experimental foundations.

Research Interests

Peptide Therapeutics

Development of computational methods for predicting and designing therapeutic peptides, including antibacterial, cell-penetrating, and other bioactive peptides. My work focuses on identifying functional peptides and predicting their biological activities to support drug discovery.

Aptamer Therapeutics

Development of curated databases and computational resources for DNA and RNA aptamers with applications in cancer, bacterial, and viral diseases. My research aims to facilitate the development of therapeutic aptamer-based classification tools.

Biological Databases

Building manually curated biological databases and knowledgebases that organize experimentally validated information on peptides, proteins, and nucleic-acid therapeutics.

Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Research

Exploring the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and large language models to address challenges in bioinformatics, biomolecular prediction, and therapeutic development.

Machine Learning in Bioinformatics

Development of machine learning and deep learning models for biological sequence analysis, integrating sequence-derived features, evolutionary information, and protein language model embeddings to address diverse biological problems.

Therapeutic Discovery

Application of computational biology and artificial intelligence to accelerate the discovery of peptide-, protein-, and aptamer-based therapeutics for biomedical research.

Web Server & Software Development

Creating freely accessible web servers and standalone software that enable researchers to analyze biological sequences and use predictive models through intuitive interfaces.
