Artificial Intelligence for Therapeutic Discovery

My research focuses on peptide- and aptamer-based therapeutics with a little bit of interest in phages. The objective is to create reliable, accessible computational resources that support biomedical research and therapeutic discovery.

ANTIBACTERIAL PEPTIDES

AntiBP3

AntiBP3 is a machine learning-based prediction server for identifying antibacterial peptides active against Gram-positive, Gram-negative, and Gram-variable bacteria. The framework integrates sequence-derived features and hybrid prediction strategies to facilitate antibacterial peptide discovery.

Visit AntiBP3 →

PEPTIDE THERAPEUTICS

EIPpred

EIPpred predicts inhibitory peptides against Escherichia coli and estimates their minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC). The server assists researchers in discovering and optimizing peptide therapeutics with desired antibacterial activity.

Visit EIPpred →

CELL-PENETRATING PEPTIDES

CPPsite3

CPPsite3 is an updated repository of experimentally validated cell-penetrating peptides containing detailed information on peptide sequences, cargo molecules, chemical modifications, uptake efficiency, and experimental evidence.

Visit CPPsite3 →

CPP Subcellular Localization

CPPLocPred

CPPLocPred is a hierarchical machine learning framework for identifying cell-penetrating peptides and predicting their subcellular localization using sequence-derived features and artificial intelligence.

Visit CPPLocPred →

APTAMER DATABASES

AptCancerDB

A manually curated database of experimentally validated anticancer aptamers containing information on aptamer sequences, targets, binding affinity, chemical modifications, cancer types, applications, and associated literature.

Visit AptCancerDB →


AptViralDB

A comprehensive resource of experimentally validated antiviral aptamers designed to facilitate research on viral diagnostics and therapeutics. Published in Molecular Therapy — Nucleic Acids (2026).

Visit AptViralDB →


AptBacterialDB

A curated database of antibacterial aptamers providing experimentally validated information on bacterial targets, aptamer sequences, applications, and binding characteristics.

Visit AptBacterialDB →

PHAGE THERAPY

Computational Resources for Phage Therapy

My research also focuses on bacteriophage-based therapeutics and the computational resources that support phage discovery, characterization, engineering, and therapeutic applications. This work includes a comprehensive review of bioinformatics databases, prediction tools, and machine learning approaches developed for phage-based therapy and antimicrobial research.

CURRENT RESEARCH

AntiCapt

AntiCapt predicts, designs, and scans anticancer single-stranded DNA aptamers. It uses fine-tuned HyenaDNA nucleotide language model embeddings with LightGBM classifiers, and includes two models: one separating anticancer aptamers from random oligonucleotides (the default) and one separating them from general aptamers.

CURRENT PROJECTS

Therapeutic Aptamer Prediction

Ongoing work extending machine-learning and language-model approaches to predict therapeutic aptamers.


Pan-Cancer Genomic Map of Therapeutic Aptamer Targets

Profiles the protein targets of validated anticancer aptamers from AptCancerDB across cancer types and healthy tissues, combining genomic, expression and clinical data into a targetability score for each target–cancer pair.
